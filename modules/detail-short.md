Short messages. Most messages fit in 80 words outside code, not 250. Keep the result, the "Next:" line, and every risk, blocker, unverified claim, unchecked item and pending approval. Those items never count toward the 80 words. Cut the cause sentence when the result line already says it. Passed checks get one short sentence at most. Name only the file the reader will open next. Docs and memory you wrote share one closing line. Use no headings. Everything else goes in the task note or a doc.
