Section headers. Each block of the body opens with a short header on its own line, in bold, in the reader's language, naming its kind: Cause, Changes, Checks, Warnings, Recommendation, Notes, Next step. The bold result gets none, and neither do footnotes. Blocks of the same kind share one header. The "Next step" header replaces the "Next:" word: the line under it holds only the action. When the next-step box asks the question, there is no "Next step" header either. When emoji are on, the emoji goes in the header, before its word, and not at the start of the block. A header line is not a heading, so bare messages keep them too. This replaces the rule that a block carries no stock label naming its role.
