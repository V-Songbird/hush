Next step on top. The "Next:" line moves up. It sits right under the bold result, as its own paragraph. Its word stays in bold, in the reader's language. Code the step needs, such as a snippet to paste, may follow it directly. Then a horizontal rule, unless the rules setting is off, and the body after it. The message ends with the body. No rule and no "Next:" line at the end. Footnotes come last, after the body, with no rule above them. The app shows a footnote on the very last line as plain text. So when footnotes end the message, add one final line that holds only `&nbsp;`.
